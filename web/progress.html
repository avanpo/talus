How Talus measures cardiovascular endurance & run fitness

Aerobic Efficiency Factor (EF)

Formula: Speed (m/min) ÷ Average HR (bpm)
EF reflects stroke volume — ground covered per heartbeat (GAP-adjusted for elevation). An upward trend confirms aerobic adaptation; toggle Heat-Adjusted to strip out summer temperature drag.

Training Load & ACWR

Model: Daily HR TRIMP → 42-day Fitness (CTL), 7-day Fatigue (ATL), Form (TSB = CTL − ATL), and Ramp Ratio (ATL ÷ CTL). Keep ACWR near 0.8–1.3 to build safely.

Aerobic Decoupling & Durability

Decoupling: 1st-half vs 2nd-half cardiac drift (target < 5%).
Durability: Late-run EF (60+ min) vs fresh EF (min 5–30) on long runs ≥ 70 min.

Fatigue & Grade Normalization

Model: Minetti GAP (1m climb ≈ 7.5m flat) + In-Run Position + Acute 7-Day Load.
Normalizes EF and segment scores for cumulative fatigue and terrain.
